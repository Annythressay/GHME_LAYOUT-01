Portraits preserve the original 1350 × 1350 pixels. Logos preserve their source
colors; empty transparent margins on the Sedbergh and Elite Dental logos were trimmed.

Page copy draws on pages 4–9 and 11–18. Contact details use page 18. The page
does not treat individual credentials as institutional GHME accreditation or
reproduce the profile's unverified survival/cost statistics. No formal mission,
vision, founding year or numerical scale claims were added.
